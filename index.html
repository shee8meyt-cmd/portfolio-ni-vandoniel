01

TRIGO

Thesis · Lead developer+

TRIGO is a tricycle ride-booking app for Rizal, Palawan, with separate passenger, driver, and admin roles. Passengers drop a pickup and destination pin and see the fare before they book. Drivers accept rides and follow a road route to the passenger.

KotlinJetpack ComposeFirebaseSupabaseMapLibreOpenRouteService
  • Live driver and passenger tracking on the map
  • Fare formula fitted to local terminal rates
  • Driver verification with document upload
  • Firestore security rules with role-based access
